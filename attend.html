Airport Information

Houston has two major airports: George Bush Intercontinental Airport (IAH) and William P. Hobby Airport (HOU). IAH is approximately 25 miles from the conference venue, BioScience Research Collaborative, Rice University. HOU is approximately 10 miles from the conference venue.

Route to the Venue (For Local Attendees)

The conference will be held at BRC Rice University.

Public Transit

The venue is easily accessible via the city's public transit system and is located at BRC, Rice University.

  • Subway/Metro: Take the METRORail Red Line to the Rice University/Hermann Park Station.
    The venue is a 5-10 minute walk from the station through the campus or along Main Street.
  • Bus: Routes 27 and 56 stop near the university entrance on Main Street.

For real-time transit information, check the METRO website.

Driving and Parking

On-site parking is available for a daily fee of $12.

  • Venue Address for GPS: BRC Rice University
  • Parking Map: BRC has underground parking, and the B3 level is for visitors and conference attendees.

We encourage local attendees to utilize public transit or ridesharing to minimize traffic and parking congestion.
